SHOULD NEVER BE ENTERED.

Silent Attendens is a psychological horror mystery set inside a haunted Indian college hostel.

The player explores abandoned corridors, discovers hidden stories, investigates mysterious disappearances and tries to survive an entity that should never be awakened.

The environment is rooted in Indian hostel culture — rather than generic Western horror.

PROJECT / SILENT ATTENDENS

THE HOSTEL REMEMBERS.

A student enters an abandoned hostel where something feels wrong.

Rooms that should be empty are not.

Footsteps appear behind you.

Names are whispered from dark corridors.

Old photographs reveal forgotten events.

And somewhere inside the building... something is waiting.
